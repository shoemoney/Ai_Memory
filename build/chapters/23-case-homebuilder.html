The business and the replacement

A coordinator asks which framing-agreement version changed the notice period. A company-hosted model cites regional buyer, phone and agreement records; people make commitments. Use this design when scattered CRM, phone and paper records need regional isolation. For one jurisdiction, or a region too small for three GPU hosts, use the smaller designs in Chapters 21 and 22.

Invented Meridian Hearth Group builds in the United States, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Spain and the United Arab Emirates. Assume 450 staff, 6,000 active buyers, 2,000 subcontractor firms and 40 active projects. All models have open weights and run locally, including embeddings, translation, vision and speech.

This engineering reference design supplies no legal, compliance or tax advice. Regional counsel must confirm signature and data-protection rules before release; database tests cannot establish agreement enforceability. Signing, telephony, GPU serving and multi-region operation follow vendor documentation and were not run.

All three systems feed one context builder. The buyer CRM follows leads, reservations, home and lot selections, change orders, closing and warranty. The phone system joins reception, sales, project offices and after-hours messages, and the agreement process covers bids, scopes of work, subcontract agreements, change orders, lien waivers (releases of payment claims on the property), insurance certificates and licenses with expiry dates. Staff search separate inboxes and paper folders, sometimes quoting buyers the wrong version.

Keep CRM forms and deterministic workflows. The LLM reads authorized evidence, answers questions, drafts text, suggests routes and proposes memory updates. Named people and deterministic services maintain legal/financial records, accept contracts, release payments and validate licenses, insurance certificates and lien waivers.

Local inference cannot replace per-country phone carriers, qualified trust services (when a document requires a qualified signature), payments and banking, or public registries. Send only approved transaction data. Replacing the phone switch (PBX) still requires carrier connections (SIP trunks) for public calls.

Recommended architecture, November 2026

Five data regions with no personal-data federation

Run five deployments (US, Mexico, UK, EU and UAE), one per data region rather than one per country, with Spanish projects on the EU deployment. This engineering policy limits where personal data can go, without assuming every country requires domestic storage. EU GDPR Article 44 puts transfers under the conditions of its Chapter V, the ICO explains UK restricted transfers, and Mexico's 2025 private-sector law covers transfers in Articles 35 and 36, including privacy-notice duties and exceptions. These rules favor deciding each transfer explicitly over one global recall index.2

Unverified: UAE interpretation

The official UAE personal-data law download returned an access error, and the TDRA signature-law copy could not be fetched, so this design asserts no article-level UAE permission or signature rule. UAE personal-data export and automated agreement signing remain blocked until local counsel supplies verified requirements.3

Regions independently hold credentials, database, files, Redis, inference endpoints, encryption keys, logs and backups. Buyer/contractor-personnel data, embeddings, recordings and prompts stay regional. The gateway authenticates regional identity; area codes, language and model output cannot authorize access. Changing region requires a separately granted account; global executives gain no implicit access.

Only a reviewed, non-personal reference pack travels globally. Before adding files, strip customer annotations, plot addresses, signatures and photo metadata. The stripped source tree holds generic plans, specifications, product catalogs and clauses; regions check it out read-only under procedures/. A plan containing a buyer's choices is regional. Clauses specific to a jurisdiction stay labeled even when their source files are global. A corporate wiki index points to regional procedures without indexing regional personal data (Chapter 02).

Five separate recall endpoints remove routing errors possible with a region-tagged global index. Global reporting accepts only approved aggregate tables with small groups suppressed, never free-form regional summaries that could name people.

Hardware, serving and model decisions

Per region, budget a main host: 96 GB NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPU, 256 GB RAM, 4 TB mirrored NVMe. Add a separate 96 GB voice host and, before phone cutover, an identical warm spare: three hosts covering one failure. Only GPU capacity is manufacturer-specified; other sizing is estimated. The spare holds 27B for main failure. On voice failure, its standby Asterisk takes the secondary trunk, unloads 27B and loads 9B/STT/TTS; callers hear a recorded greeting and queue for people. Release requires a timed swap within the approved, still-unmeasured outage budget. Test mixed load with main-host ingestion behind interactive work.4

Main-host Qwen3.8-27B is pinned in region.yaml. It is a dense (non-MoE) 27B hybrid-attention vision-language model under Apache-2.0 with 262,144 native context tokens. Start with bfloat16 and no quantization, which takes about 54 GB for the weights (27 billion × 2 bytes) before the vision encoder, runtime and conversation cache. Cap context at 32,768 tokens; eight active requests is an unvalidated ceiling. The key/value (KV) attention cache grows with active sequence count/length, separately from weights. Only 16 layers use full attention; others keep fixed-size recurrent state. Four KV heads with 256-dimensional heads mean eight full contexts need 8 × 32,768 × 16 × 4 × 256 × 2 (K/V) × 2 bytes = 16 GiB (17.2 decimal GB). Weights + cache + the estimated 12 GB for embeddings/reranking/bounded page ingestion total about 83 GB of 96 GB. Mirror that reservation on the spare. Measure vision, recurrent state and runtime within the remaining 13 GB; lower concurrency if needed.5

Serve with vLLM 0.34.0 (Apache-2.0). Continuous batching combines active requests per decoding step. The tuning guide covers chunked prefill (splitting long prompt processing) and batching-token budgets across requests. The released checkpoint supports tools: its parser extracts proposals; the gateway checks schema and authorization before approval.6

Set chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=false for routine recall. The card says thinking is on by default and documents this switch. Contract comparison may use a separately queued reasoning task with a bounded output budget and human review, and its trace must never consume the live-call budget. Keep contract folders out of routine prompts even when they fit the context ceiling.5

The fallback and phone model is Qwen3.5-9B (Apache-2.0, bfloat16, about 18 GB of weights, 262,144 native context), deployed with an 8,192-token context for phones. Its card documents tool calling and the same thinking switch. Keep it loaded on the voice host. If main and spare fail, the voice host runs reception only (5,000 input and 256 output tokens), with a degraded-service notice and unchanged evidence filters; buyer-case and agreement questions queue for staff. If voice and spare fail, carriers route to people. If both primary hosts fail, the spare serves 27B and carriers route to people.7

Translation needs a test gate

Business languages are English, Spanish and Arabic. Model translations remain uncertified. Store original, translation, source hash, glossary version, reviewer and governing language together. Counsel chooses each governing-language clause without defaulting to English; English governs only these invented retrieval fixtures.

Before release, test 24 examples: eight concepts across three languages (change order, lien waiver, practical completion, retention, insurance endorsement, governing law, calendar day and working day). Retention here means withheld completion money. Include positive/near-miss sentences and bilingual-reviewed meanings. Preserve amount, negation, deadline, party and exception; require zero critical meaning errors. Same-model back-translation finds errors without providing independent validation.

Reject translations expanding one lien waiver to all claims. Keep Arabic drafting human-led until qualified bilingual reviewers approve terminology and representative contracts.

Store relationships as relational edges first, such as contractor employs person, contractor assigned to project, and agreement version governs scope. Facts and edges carry two timelines: valid time (when the claim applied) and recorded time (when the system learned it). All intervals include their start and exclude their end. A late import can change today's understanding of last month's contract without rewriting what staff knew then. Recorded-time surfaces use recorded_at (configuration/prompts), recorded_from/recorded_to (SQL interval bounds), and RECORDED (test output).

Caller assertions never directly become legal facts. “Buyer requests blue tile” and “blue tile selection accepted” are different predicates with different writers. A project diary is an episode until an authorized person confirms a schedule fact. Contradictions stay linked to their sources, and consolidation never replaces a signed version with a summary.

Retrieval with mandatory boundaries

The gateway binds region to caller identity and obtains project grants/role from authorization, enforcing them in SQL, keyword/vector search, reranking, file fetches and caches. Asking for a Mexican buyer’s phone cannot widen US grants. Database non-owner roles cannot bypass row-level security (RLS, Chapter 08); file fetches repeat checks. Recheck recalled/retained evidence against current grants before reuse. Compose recall keys from authorization scope, grant revision, query valid/recorded window and a cache-generation counter advanced on supersession/deletion. Test a repeated question after warming caches and revoking its project grant: exclude cached passages and prior-turn evidence.

For prose questions, retrieve at most 20 keyword and 20 vector candidates within grants (Chapter 05). Merge ranks with reciprocal rank fusion (RRF), rerank the top 20 and pack at most six passages within budget. Filter superseded versions by valid/recorded time before ranking; historical windows retain access filters.

Without admissible sources, return “I found no authorized source for that question” and offer human handoff; general model knowledge cannot substitute. Conflicting clauses or a low reranker score require the same handoff, showing competing sources. Calibrate the cutoff on stage 1’s labeled answerable/unanswerable questions; raw cosine is not a refusal threshold.

The deterministic agreement flow

The contract manager selects jurisdiction, document class, parties and scope; the model proposes a template version, approved clauses, exceptions and source identifiers. The manager approves the clause set, and the model fills a draft from structured scope data. Counsel approves the governing-language clause and negotiated deviations; the bilingual reviewer approves the translation. Both language versions retain independent hashes and one linked review decision.

Only the verifier creates signed receipts, checking envelope identity, expected hash, completed signers, evidence package and signature policy. It rejects unsigned, stale, substituted or replayed payloads. A retry reuses the verified artifact; a reconciliation job removes unreferenced files under the retention policy. Models lack signing access.

Phones: disclosure, consent and caller identity

Consider pinned Asterisk 24.0.0; verify its release and GPL version 2 license in the official repository. Connect approved country SIP trunks and send media through documented AudioSocket to the regional voice application. Staff keep direct non-AI routing for ordinary and emergency calls.18

Reception discloses AI on every regional bot call and offers a reviewed language menu. EU AI Act Article 50(1) requires people to be told when they interact with an AI system, subject to its stated exception. Do not rely on that exception to skip this disclosure. Article 113 in the official text gives the general application date of 2 August 2026. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (published 24 July 2026) moves the high-risk dates to 2 December 2027 and 2 August 2028 and leaves Article 50(1) unchanged.19

Recording consent is a deterministic state machine that runs before any stored audio or transcription. Counsel supplies a maintained caller and callee rule table, and the service applies the strictest applicable requirement; an area code is not reliable evidence of location. The service requests consent, offers unrecorded human routing and retains permitted consent evidence. US federal law has a one-party-consent exception, which does not show that a call meets every state or foreign rule. No all-country recording-law matrix was verified.20

The dialed number selects a regional service without granting data access. Case access requires verified caller identity and regional case grant. Caller ID can be spoofed, so it only proposes a record: the agent sends a one-time code to the phone or email already on it. Only a code verified against that contact method, followed by a successful case-grant check, unlocks get_buyer_case. The agent retrieves a short history and permitted project facts, routes the call or takes a message, and proposes a provisional summary (call ID, consent state, transcript pointer, uncertain names and dates) for the regional communications service to commit. People confirm closing changes and legal commitments. If identity is uncertain or the grant does not match the region, the agent takes a callback message without reading private records aloud.

Use short-sentence synthesis. Voice activity detection on the caller stream stops synthesis and clears queued playback at sustained speech. Measure the 1.5-second response target from the end of the caller’s correction; test partial-word playback and half-spoken corrections at the handset. Bound live calls separately from batch summaries, and make the fallback greeting and human queue work with the LLM offline. Long agreement analysis belongs in a staff follow-up, not a phone turn. English and Spanish AI conversation stays conditional on handset and noisy-site tests.

Gotcha: patching is a release gate

Check CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog for Asterisk and its dependencies before go-live. Never expose an inherited unpatched PBX. Read Asterisk's security advisories and record their disposition for the pinned build and its dependencies.21

What breaks first and when to move

Downsize to Chapter 22’s one-host 9B design, with people answering phones, after peak demand including prefill meets that design’s latency gates through a full project cycle and the 9B passes clause-answer fixtures. The assumed 40 tokens/second on the 27B does not establish 9B capacity; no fixed downgrade threshold was measured.

Inference queues and long prefills fail first, especially when plans and bilingual drafts share the main host's GPU with staff chat. Add or split inference capacity when p95 queue wait exceeds 300 ms at normal peaks, when the voice objective fails, or when the bounded context cache rejects the target concurrency. Use the spare for batch work only if a failover drill shows those jobs can be interrupted and the GPU reclaimed within the approved outage budget; otherwise keep it idle and add a replica for batch.

Start pgvector in the regional transactional store and measure exact versus approximate retrieval on a labeled question set, taking exact scans over 1.2 million chunks as the baseline. Then add a suitable vector index on the pinned pgvector version and test filtered recall before moving databases (Chapter 08). Consider a dedicated vector store when page multi-vectors are required, or when search competes with CRM writes and p95 recall exceeds a chosen 300 ms budget after tuning (Chapter 11). Keep SQL as the record and propagate deletions (Chapter 17).

Risks and limits

A model can misread a clause. A correct page citation can accompany a reversed exception. Show the source text beside the answer, test negation and precedence, and require a contract manager for any commitment.

Old clauses can look relevant. Filter valid/recorded time and approval before ranking: similarity cannot establish currency. Never merge versions into one summary or infer acceptance from email. Ask the contract manager for missing amendment evidence.

Translation can change the obligation. Executed translations need bilingual review (see the terminology gate above), and a good Spanish retrieval result says nothing about Arabic contract quality.

Cross-border leakage can occur outside the vector query. Traces, monitoring payloads, caches, email exports and remote support sessions can carry personal data too, so the regional policy covers them. Test with distinct synthetic canaries in each region and project at every interface, not only SQL.

Staff can over-trust fluency. Show uncertainty, source date and review status, and audit which evidence entered the prompt and which human approved the action.
