# Experiment ledger

Every experiment, including the failures and the nulls, with its state and its result. Experiment numbers are stable references used by the write-ups; letters mark follow-ups of the same experiment. Raw result files named in the Result column live in `results/` directories that are not part of this tree; the write-ups in this tree carry the numbers, and raw traces for the fine-tuning studies are in `research/tinker/` and `research/tools-trained/`.

Dollar figures are token-based estimates at list prices unless stated otherwise. "Mac Studio" is a Mac Studio M3 Ultra with 96 GB of unified memory; "stock" is Inkling 2 Mini in a mixed-precision 4-bit build (oQ4e) served with oMLX.
