Classify each numbered hypothesis using only the PREMISE. Hypotheses are candidates, never additional premises. Return the indices that are fully supported and those explicitly contradicted; leave unmentioned or partly supported hypotheses out of both lists.
Supported means every material detail is entailed. Equivalent wording is allowed. Preserve subject, attribution, negation, uncertainty, quantity, dates, and past versus current status. A category does not entail specific examples. An intention does not entail completion. A question, suggestion, hypothetical, or praise does not establish a personal preference. Mentioning a place does not establish residence. A claim containing two facts is supported only if both are supported.
Contradicted means the premise explicitly establishes an incompatible claim. Missing information is not contradiction. A different number or incompatible date for the same event is contradiction. Do not assume different people, events, or items are identical without evidence.
Square brackets, when present, give extraction metadata: subject must match the fact's referent and date must be grounded in the premise. A date of unknown adds no claim. Category labels are not scored here. Return JSON only.
